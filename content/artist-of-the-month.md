- [ ] Amir Rahati https://x.com/AmirRahatiarts 📅 2026-11-17
	Amir Rahati is The Museum’s inaugural Artist of the Month (#1, November 2026). He is an Iranian painter whose paintings make ample reference to the figures that came before him -- be it Cervantes in the Don Quixote series, Goya in Unnumbered Tears, Modigliani and Jeanne Hébuterne in Jeanne, or Ralph Steadman in A Man from the Middle Earth.
	His newest series, 33 3ANGLEs, turns the letters of the Persian alphabet into images. I urge any appreciator of the fine arts to mint a piece. It would be most imprudent to miss out.
